Method & notes

Coverage. All season events on tournamentsoftware were scanned; the report keeps the 3 tournaments on the official ranking list (local pool, school and club-internal events award no ranking points and are excluded, medals included). Tervasulka medalled in 3 of them.

Roster. Built from tournament entry lists (club = Tervasulka / “TeSu”), so it includes everyone who competed, not just medalists. Players whose registration ended on an event’s exclude or reserve list (never in a draw) are not counted as participants; no-shows in a draw are. 0 medal rows come from tournaments whose entry list showed nationality only (marked °); those clubs were matched against the season roster.

Ranking points. Taken from each player’s official ranking page, counting only results from this season’s tournaments (ISO weeks 2026-W27 … 2027-W26); the official total uses a longer rolling window. All divisions are included — open, junior (U-age) and senior/veteran (35+…). Identical rows that re-rank the same result across class lists are de-duplicated, and every total is cross-checked against the per-tournament points pages of the ranking publication (which also recover players whose own ranking page is missing results).

Podium. Gold/Silver/Bronze = 1st/2nd/3rd (incl. tied 3rd/4th); round-robin 4th+ excluded. All age/class categories included.
